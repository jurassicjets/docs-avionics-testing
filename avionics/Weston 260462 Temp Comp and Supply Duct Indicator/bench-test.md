bench testing the Weston 260462 Temp Comp and Supply Duct Indicator

_________________________________
**EQUIPMENT REQUIRED**

DC power supply, variable voltage source (potentiometer, digipot, decade box, etc.)

_________________________________
**TEST SETUP**

Connect 28v power. Connect ground and signal references to ground. Connect signal wires to ground via variable resistance sources. Suggest using high resolution resistance sources as the operating range is quite narrow.
